## A page

- One topic per file, about 120 lines at most, in the right root's folder.
- Frontmatter: {{the keys, e.g. `title`, `description`, `tags` (three to seven, lowercase-hyphenated)}}.
- Opens with a two-to-four sentence summary that answers the page's question.
- Added to {{NAV_MANIFEST}} in reading order; removed from it when deleted, with every link to it fixed.
- Tables for facts, prose for reasoning, bullets for real lists; the UI's own words in **bold**.
- Components: {{the closed list, e.g. `Callout`, `Steps`, `Tabs`, `Figure`; or "plain markdown only"}}.
- Voice: {{the project's voice}}; no em-dashes, no emoji, no hype words.
- Every command, flag, default, and example was run or read from the built product before it was written.

## The same change

A change that alters what a reader sees or depends on updates its pages and retakes its shots in that commit or
pull request, and says so in one line: `Docs: <pages touched>` or `Docs: none, <why>`. The surface map in
`.agents/docs-parity.json` names the pages and capture group for each area of the code; when the map is wrong,
fix it in the same change.

# Changelog

- **0.3.0**: Any subject, drawn from the subject. A round is now a set of named views (`draft new --views transcript,lanes,layers=layer-stack`): each draft starts on a blank stage unless it names a study whose view is the view, states what it draws in `view.json` before it draws it, and `draft check` fails one that has not, or that is still the blank stage or the study as shipped. The shipped figures are studies to learn technique from (`starters/` is `studies/`, `draft archetypes` is `draft studies`, `--archetypes` is gone), with two new ones: `time-grid` and `cell-field`. The kit gains `flat/`: `Panel`, `Rows`, `Bar`, `Spawn`, `Elbow`, `Token`, `Queue`, `Meter`, `Cells`, `Lanes`, and the geometry they are laid out with; `Label` takes a page point. A model may say what is inside what (`parent`), what comes into being (`born`), what repeats (`repeat`), numbers that change (`quantities`, read with `f.level` and `f.share`) and the data a view lays out (`data`); every earlier model still passes. `lenses.md` says how two lenses share a picture, and the review checklist tests for a template with new labels.
- **0.2.0**: A 3D tier. Two new archetypes, `iso-flow-3d` and `layer-stack-3d`, draw the same model on React Three Fiber: clay, matte black, frosted glass and the accent, soft shadows, flat labels over the canvas, driven by the same clock. The kit gains `three/`, copied into a repo only with `kit install --three` or by landing a 3D draft; the 2D tier still depends on React alone. The studio carries three.js, so 3D drafts and stills add nothing to a repo, and stills are drawn in software so they are the same on every machine. The `visualize` skill asks before a 3D figure adds packages to a site. Also two more README exports from `pt still`, both made by holding the figure's clock and photographing it, never by recording. `--strip` writes the told beats in one row, numbered, each with its caption (`<name>.strip.light.png`, `<name>.strip.dark.png`). `--gif` writes one loop as `<name>.gif` (`--dark` adds a dark one, `--fps` sets the rate): 720 px wide at 15 frames a second unless told otherwise, its size printed after every export, a warning past 5 MB, and nothing written past GitHub's 10 MB. The studio gains `sharp` to encode the GIF, so `setup --yes` runs once more after updating. `still` with neither flag does what it did.
- **0.1.0**: First release. Replaces `animated-storytelling`, which explained ideas as decks; this one draws systems. Two skills: `visualize` (model the system, draft three variations, refine one, deliver a React component for MDX) and `export-stills` (light and dark PNGs for a README). A figure starts from a lens (structure, path, time, field, transformation, difference, cases, scale), chosen by what hides the mechanism, so the picture fits the subject and is not always boxes and arrows. Ships a copy-in kit, eight starters, a studio that renders drafts and stills, and the `pt` CLI. The contrast checker carries over as `scripts/contrast-check.mjs`.

# Changelog

- **0.2.0**: Two more README exports from `pt still`, both made by holding the figure's clock and photographing it, never by recording. `--strip` writes the told beats in one row, numbered, each with its caption (`<name>.strip.light.png`, `<name>.strip.dark.png`). `--gif` writes one loop as `<name>.gif` (`--dark` adds a dark one, `--fps` sets the rate): 720 px wide at 15 frames a second unless told otherwise, its size printed after every export, a warning past 5 MB, and nothing written past GitHub's 10 MB. The studio gains `sharp` to encode the GIF, so `setup --yes` runs once more after updating. `still` with neither flag does what it did.
- **0.1.0**: First release. Replaces `animated-storytelling`, which explained ideas as decks; this one draws systems. Two skills: `visualize` (model the system, draft three variations, refine one, deliver a React component for MDX) and `export-stills` (light and dark PNGs for a README). A figure starts from a lens (structure, path, time, field, transformation, difference, cases, scale), chosen by what hides the mechanism, so the picture fits the subject and is not always boxes and arrows. Ships a copy-in kit, eight starters, a studio that renders drafts and stills, and the `pt` CLI. The contrast checker carries over as `scripts/contrast-check.mjs`.

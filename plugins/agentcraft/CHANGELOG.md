# Changelog

- **0.1.0**: The `start-here` skill, on its own. Typed with no argument it asks which areas a person wants in up to four grouped questions, explains each one chosen, asks how they want to use it, and hands off to the skill that does the work. Given a task, or with nobody to ask, it asks nothing and names the area and skill that fit. The areas are the twelve agentic-engineering plugins, and a hand-off goes to one of their skills by its address; a skill only a person can start is named for them to type.
